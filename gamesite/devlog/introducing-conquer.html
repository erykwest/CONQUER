Introducing CONQUER.

A strategy project built around a simple ambition: make the land and the people matter.

CONQUER begins with a medieval territory. Its geography shapes where settlements can grow, how resources are reached and what makes a fortification useful. The aim is to connect these systems rather than to build an elaborate but decorative scene.

One world, connected rules

The Builder prototype focuses on landscape, roads, construction and a growing settlement. Castle geometry is not just an ornament: walls, gates and built sections are designed to become meaningful in defense and conflict.

A smaller, clearer economy

Instead of endless production chains, the design calls for a compact resource economy with local specialization. A farming settlement and a mining community should look and behave differently because of where they are.

Prototype before promises

Some systems already exist in the repository and others are in active development. The public Builder will be an experimental singleplayer experience, not the full planned multiplayer kingdom.

Simulation first. A more expressive world follows.

Development updates will track decisions and visible progress, and distinguish implemented features from future goals.
