Your data

Privacy and transparency.

This notice describes the intended data flows of the CONQUER website and browser prototype. It will be finalized with the controller identity and production service details before public launch.

Guest play

Guest games are stored in your browser's local storage, under separate game slots. These records can be lost if you clear browser data or use private browsing. Playing as Guest does not enroll you in a multiplayer campaign or create an account.

Player account

When you request an email sign-in link, the account provider processes your email for authentication and session management. The game and website use one account identity, while access to any future private multiplayer data requires separate authorization.

Development email subscription

Following the project is optional, uses a confirmation email and is separate from account creation. We process the subscribed email address and the status of confirmation or withdrawal only to deliver selected CONQUER updates. You can unsubscribe using the link provided in emails.

Analytics

No third-party advertising trackers are installed by this website. If anonymous aggregate traffic measurement is introduced, this page will be updated with the actual provider and retention policy.

Your rights and contact

Before publication, the project must identify its data controller and publish the correct contact details, retention policy, processors and transfer information. For development-stage correspondence, use the contact page.

Draft notice: Not a final legal disclosure. Public subscription launch must remain disabled until controller details, service contracts and retention periods are supplied.
